Rooted in the neighbourhood

A practice with
a local history.

1983

Our story began in Jurong East, founded by Dr Chiam Tok Joon and Dr Chee Ai Guat.

Today, Eastern Dental Surgery serves patients across three neighbourhood clinics and is a member of Quantum Healthcare.

  1. 1983

    Jurong East

    The first clinic opened, sharing its premises with a medical clinic.

  2. 1985

    Bukit Batok

    The practice expanded to Bukit Batok. Dr Peh Gek Yock joined the team.

  3. 1987

    Jurong West

    A third clinic gave patients another neighbourhood location to visit.

Before your visit

A little clarity
before you call.

Where are the clinics?

Our three locations are at Jurong East Street 32, Bukit Batok Street 21 and Jurong West Avenue 1. Full addresses, opening hours and directions are listed in the clinic section.

How do I request an appointment?

Call or WhatsApp your chosen clinic using the details above. You can also use the existing online request form. Your appointment is confirmed only when the clinic confirms the date and time.

Can I check treatment fees before visiting?

Please contact your clinic about consultation fees, treatment estimates and payment options. No unverified prices or subsidy amounts are quoted on this page.

Can I request a particular dentist?

Contact the clinic and mention the dentist you would like to see. The team can advise on availability before confirming your appointment.
