# Eastern Dental Surgery — Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

Eastern Dental Surgery is a Singapore dental group established in 1983, serving patients and families through clinics in Jurong East, Bukit Batok and Jurong West. Services include routine care, orthodontics, restorative dentistry, gum treatment, wisdom tooth surgery and implants.

The redesigned homepage highlights the practice’s history and locations, presents verified clinic details and provides direct appointment-request routes.

## Three Main Improvements

### 1. Verified Clinic Contact Information

Verified addresses, telephone numbers, WhatsApp contacts and directions are presented for all three clinics. This gives patients accurate information instead of relying on the existing Contact page, which contains unrelated “Walker Construction Co.” content and San Francisco addresses.

### 2. Clearer Opening Message

The opening section introduces family dental care, names all three neighbourhood locations and highlights “Since 1983”. These facts were previously underplayed in the hero. Visitors can now understand the practice’s services, local presence and history immediately.

### 3. Clinic-Specific Appointment Requests

Each clinic has a separate WhatsApp appointment-request link, supported by direct calls and two persistent mobile contact actions. Previously, all “Book Now” buttons led to the same appointment page, requiring patients to select their clinic again. The revised links carry the clinic choice through to the enquiry.

## Design Improvements

- **Visual hierarchy:** Services, locations and practice history take priority in the opening section.
- **Consistency:** Verified details and clearly identified contact routes organise information across all three clinics.
- **User experience:** Clinic-specific links remove repeated clinic selection.
- **Mobile access:** Two persistent contact actions remain available while browsing.
